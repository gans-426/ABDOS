ABDOS

Support

ABDOS is a day planner built by someone with ADHD, for minds that do not fit ordinary to-do apps.

Get in touch

Questions, problems or ideas: write to abd86.dev@outlook.com. Please say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Keep a copy of your data

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. To keep a copy somewhere else:

  1. Open Settings in ABDOS, then Backup.
  2. Press Export backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive.

To bring it back, use Import backup on the same page.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications for ABDOS are allowed in the iPhone's own Settings, under Notifications. In ABDOS, open Settings, then Notifications, to choose when warnings and the morning report come.

Prayer times

Prayer times are off until you switch them on. Open Settings, then Prayer times, and choose your city or enter your place by hand. ABDOS does not use your location.

The Desk and the Study tab

The Desk is a companion program for a computer. Pairing one adds the Study tab to the phone. The Desk is not available to download yet; everything else in ABDOS works without it.

Not medical advice

ABDOS and its Guide are general information. They do not diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to your doctor about your own care.
