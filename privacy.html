ABDOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ABDOS is an iPhone app, with an Apple Watch app and widgets, made by Abdul Razak Alfares.

What ABDOS collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Its maker receives none of what you enter.

Where your data is kept

On your iPhone. Your tasks, habits, focus sessions, rules, meals and everything else you enter are saved in the app's own storage on the phone. If iCloud Backup is switched on, Apple includes the app's data in your phone's backup, under Apple's own terms.

Backups you make

The app can export one backup file. It goes where you choose to save it, and nowhere else.

The Desk

ABDOS can be paired with the Desk, a companion program on a computer of your own. If you pair one, the phone and that computer exchange your data directly, over your own network. Nothing passes through a server of the app's maker. If you never pair a Desk, the app sends nothing anywhere.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications, to remind you of tasks, habits, focus sessions, prayer times and your morning report.
  • Calendar, to show your calendar's events on your day. The app only reads them and never changes your calendar.
  • Local network, only to find a Desk you have paired.

ABDOS does not ask for your location. For prayer times you choose a city or enter a place by hand.

Feedback and crash reports

If you send feedback or a crash report through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple passes it to the maker, with the device details Apple attaches. That is the only way anything reaches the maker, and only when you choose to send it.

The Guide

The Guide inside the app is general information about ADHD, autism, habits, focus and sleep, with references to the published work it draws on. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to your doctor about your own care.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
